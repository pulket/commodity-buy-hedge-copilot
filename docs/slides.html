AI Commodity Copilot  ·  The solution, in one picture
Two decision tracks — Financial and Supply-Chain — fused through one Sense → Score → Decide → Act engine
One key message: timing, hedging and supply-continuity are one governed decision, not three.
Problem
Commodity buys are made in silos — Finance guesses timing, Supply Chain fire-fights stock-outs — so cost and risk swing.
→
Insight
The cheapest, safest buy needs both lenses at once: market economics and demand / inventory reality.
→
Solution
A copilot that runs both tracks through four stages and resolves them into one risk-adjusted, governed plan.
